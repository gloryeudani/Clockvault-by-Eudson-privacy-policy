Refund Policy for ClockVault by Eudson

Last Updated: October 13, 2026

Thank you for choosing ClockVault by Eudson ("we," "our," or "us"). We want to ensure you have a transparent and seamless experience with our services and digital products.

Please read this policy carefully to understand how cancellations, returns, and refund requests are processed for purchases made through our platform.

1. Merchant of Record & Processing

All web-based payments, orders, and checkout transactions for ClockVault by Eudson are processed securely by Paddle.com Market Limited ("Paddle"), our Merchant of Record (MoR).

When you purchase a subscription or digital product, your transaction is managed directly by Paddle. RevenueCat is used strictly as an underlying subscription management platform to track your subscription state and active entitlements.

2. General Refund Policy

Because our product is a digital service delivered immediately upon purchase:

  • Initial Purchase / Standard Refund Window: We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for first-time subscriptions or digital product purchases. If you are unsatisfied with your purchase within 14 days of the transaction date, you are eligible for a full refund.
  • Exceptions / Non-Refundable Items:
    • Refund requests made after the 14-day window are generally non-refundable, except where required by applicable consumer law.
    • Partial billing periods or unused time within an active subscription period are non-refundable.
3. Subscription Renewals & Cancellations
  • Automatic Renewal: All recurring subscriptions (monthly or annual) automatically renew at the end of each billing cycle unless canceled prior to the renewal date.
  • How to Cancel: You can cancel your subscription at any time via:
    • Your Account / Billing Settings in the website.
    • The Paddle Management Link provided in your purchase/renewal email confirmation receipt.
  • Cancellation Effect: Canceling stops future recurring charges. You will retain access to your subscription benefits through the end of your current paid billing period. Cancellations do not automatically trigger a refund for previously billed charges.
4. How to Request a Refund

If you meet the criteria for a refund within the 14-day window (or under special statutory rights), you may initiate a refund request in one of two ways:

  • Directly via Paddle (Recommended):
    • Use the Paddle Order Assist tool or the "Manage Order" link located at the bottom of your email receipt sent by Paddle.
    • Contact Paddle Support directly at https://paddle.net.
  • Via Our Support Team:
    • Send an email to contacteudson@gmail.com with the subject line "Refund Request - [Your Order ID or Email]".
    • Please include your order ID (found on your Paddle receipt) and the email address associated with your account.

Once approved, refunds are processed by Paddle back to the original payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, etc.). Processing times typically range between 3 to 10 business days depending on your bank or payment issuer.

5. Revocation of Entitlements

Upon approval and processing of a refund:

  • Your entitlement to access the premium features will be immediately revoked across all your devices via our integration with RevenueCat.
  • Any downloaded premium assets or offline content access tied to the subscription will be disabled.
6. Chargebacks & Disputes

If you experience an issue with your charge, we strongly encourage you to contact our support team or Paddle Support before filing a dispute or chargeback with your bank. Chargebacks can result in account suspension pending dispute resolution.

7. Statutory Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy limits or affects your statutory consumer rights under applicable local laws (e.g., EU Right of Withdrawal). If local consumer protection laws mandate a different refund or withdrawal period, those legal rights shall apply.
