ClockVault by Eudson respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the ClockVault by Eudson mobile application (the "App").

1. DATA COLLECTION AND STORAGE

ClockVault by Eudson is designed as an offline-first vault application. Your personal files are not uploaded to or stored on ClockVault servers. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal files or user data.

Vault Files
Photos, videos, audio files, documents, and other files that you import into ClockVault by Eudson are stored locally on your device according to the application's vault functionality. ClockVault by Eudson does not upload the contents of your vault files to our servers.

PINs and App Settings
Your vault PINs, preferences, configurations, token balance, and other locally required application data are stored locally on your device. You are responsible for maintaining access to your device and any credentials required to access your vault.

2. DEVICE PERMISSIONS AND SYSTEM AUTHORIZATIONS

To function as an offline secure vault, protect private media, prevent unauthorized access, and manage user-selected files, ClockVault by Eudson requests specific Android permissions. We only request permissions strictly necessary for core application functionality. All data accessed remains on your device and is never uploaded or transmitted to external servers.

A. Device Permissions (User-Prompted)

1. POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Show Notifications — Android 13+)

  • Purpose: Displays real-time progress indicators for vault file import and restore operations, alerts you when batch operations complete or are cancelled, and notifies you when an intruder break-in attempt is recorded.

2. CAMERA (Take Pictures and Record Video — android.permission.CAMERA)

  • Purpose: Powers the Break-In / Intruder Selfie security feature. When enabled, the front-facing camera captures a silent photo of any unauthorized person who enters an incorrect vault PIN on the secret disguise clock. All captured intruder photos are encrypted and stored solely within your local device vault and are never transmitted to any third party or remote server.

3. READ_MEDIA_IMAGES (Read Images in Shared Storage — Android 13+)

  • Purpose: Enables you to browse, select, and import photos and gallery image files into the encrypted vault so they are hidden from other gallery and photo-viewing applications on your device.

4. READ_MEDIA_VIDEO (Read Videos in Shared Storage — Android 13+)

  • Purpose: Enables you to browse, select, and import video recordings into the private vault for secure, offline playback inside the built-in video player.

5. READ_MEDIA_AUDIO (Read Audio in Shared Storage — Android 13+)

  • Purpose: Enables you to browse, select, import, and listen to voice memos, music, and audio recordings privately inside the encrypted vault audio player.

6. READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Read Shared Storage — Android 12 & Below)

  • Purpose: On devices running Android 12 or lower, provides read access to user-selected media and documents to facilitate import into the private vault.

7. WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Write Shared Storage — Android 10 & Below)

  • Purpose: Used on legacy Android devices to restore unhidden files back to public storage folders (DCIM, Pictures, Documents, Download) and delete the original unencrypted file after vault import.

B. Special Access (All-Files Access)

MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (All-Files Access — Android 11+)

  • Purpose: ClockVault by Eudson is a comprehensive private security vault and file locker app. In order to fulfill its core purpose of protecting arbitrary file types (including PDFs, documents, spreadsheets, zip archives, custom file types, and media) and performing atomic file relocations, deletions from public storage after import, and full file restoration back to their original file system directories without data corruption, all-files access is essential for full vault functionality on modern Android versions.
  • User Control & Privacy: ClockVault by Eudson only accesses files that you explicitly choose to import, hide, delete, or restore. ClockVault by Eudson never scans, indexes, or transmits your personal files to any external server or third party.

Permission Control & Revocation
All permissions are optional, requested with transparent user consent, and can be revoked or modified at any time through your device's System Settings > Apps > ClockVault by Eudson > Permissions. Disabling certain permissions (such as All Files Access or Camera) may limit specific features like original file deletion or the Intruder Selfie mechanism.

4. IN-APP PURCHASES, SUBSCRIPTIONS & RESTORE PURCHASES

ClockVault by Eudson may offer membership plans and other paid features. When you make a purchase or subscription, transactions are processed securely through Paystack (for payment processing and billing infrastructure). ClockVault by Eudson does not receive, process, or store your complete payment-card details.

Restore Purchases: Users may restore active subscription plans and Pro feature entitlements on their devices via verified email confirmation or transaction reference. Please note that while active membership subscriptions and non-consumable feature entitlements (such as Pro features and custom themes) are restored, consumable in-app token balances earned through daily activity, ad completions, or promotional allocations are stored locally on the device and may not be restored alongside membership plans during a Restore Purchase operation following device reinstallation or data clearance.

5. TOKENS AND MEMBERSHIP ENTITLEMENTS

ClockVault by Eudson provides in-app tokens (C tokens) and membership entitlements to unlock features, theme customizations, and tools. Token balances and entitlement information are stored locally in secure device storage. Because tokens are consumable in-app utility credits, they are bound to the local application session on your device and are non-transferable, non-refundable, and cannot be converted into real-world currency.

ClockVault by Eudson may use trusted third-party services to provide advertising, in-app purchases, or core technical functionality:

Each third-party provider collects and processes information in accordance with their respective privacy policies. We encourage users to review these policies directly.

7. DATA RETENTION AND USER DELETION RIGHTS

Because ClockVault by Eudson operates offline and stores vault files and settings strictly on your local device:

  • You have complete control over all stored files and data at all times.
  • You may restore files back to public storage or delete them permanently from within the vault at any time.
  • Clearing the application's data or uninstalling the app permanently deletes all locally encrypted vault data, configuration settings, and intruder logs from the device.
