Refund Policy for ClockVault by Eudson

Last Updated: October 2026

Thank you for choosing ClockVault by Eudson ("we," "our," or "us"). We want to ensure you have a transparent and seamless experience with our services and digital products.

Please read this policy carefully to understand how cancellations, returns, and refund requests are processed for purchases made through our platform.

1. Payment Processing

All online payments, subscription checkouts, and transaction processing for ClockVault by Eudson are securely facilitated by Paystack, our payment gateway partner.

When you purchase a subscription or digital feature unlock, your payment is processed via Paystack's secure payment infrastructure. Eudson Studio does not store or process your complete credit/debit card numbers or sensitive bank details.

2. General Refund Policy

Because our product is a digital software service delivered immediately upon successful transaction completion:

  • Initial Purchase / Standard Refund Window: We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for initial subscription purchases or Pro feature upgrades. If you experience technical issues or are unsatisfied with your purchase within 14 days of the transaction date, you are eligible to request a full refund.
  • Exceptions / Non-Refundable Items:
    • Refund requests made after the 14-day window are non-refundable, except where required by applicable law.
    • Partial billing cycles or unused subscription time after active usage are non-refundable.
    • In-app consumable token balances (C tokens) earned or used within the application are non-refundable and non-transferable.
3. Subscription Renewals & Cancellations
  • Automatic Renewal: Recurring subscriptions (monthly or annual) automatically renew at the end of each billing cycle unless canceled prior to the renewal date.
  • How to Cancel: You can cancel your subscription at any time by contacting our support team or using the subscription management link provided in your email receipt.
  • Cancellation Effect: Canceling prevents future recurring charges. You will retain access to your Pro subscription benefits through the end of your current paid billing period. Cancellations do not automatically issue a refund for previously billed charges.
4. How to Request a Refund

If you meet the criteria for a refund within the 14-day eligibility window, you can submit a request directly to our support team:

  • Send an email to contacteudson@gmail.com with the subject line "Refund Request - [Your Order Reference or Email]".
  • Please include your Paystack Transaction Reference (found on your payment receipt) and the email address used during checkout.

Once evaluated and approved by Eudson Studio, refunds are initiated directly through Paystack back to your original payment method (Bank Card, Bank Transfer, USSD, or Mobile Money). Refunded funds typically reflect in your account within 3 to 10 business days, depending on your financial institution.

5. Revocation of Entitlements

Upon approval and processing of a refund:

  • Your Pro membership status and entitlement access to premium features will be revoked on your account.
  • Any premium theme customizations or subscriber-only features linked to the refunded transaction will be disabled.
6. Chargebacks & Payment Disputes

If you experience any billing discrepancies or issues with a charge, we strongly encourage you to reach out to us at contacteudson@gmail.com before filing a dispute or chargeback with your bank or card issuer. We aim to resolve all legitimate payment inquiries quickly and directly.
